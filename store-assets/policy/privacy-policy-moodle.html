Privacy Policy

Vector Learning App (the "App")
Effective date: 16 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Vector Learning App collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The App is operated by Meetcs Pvt Ltd. ("we", "us"), and connects to our learning platform at https://vector.meetcs.com/learning/ (the "Learning Site"). By using the App you agree to this policy.

Summary

The App is a mobile window onto the Learning Site. It does not send your personal data to any separate server of its own: your account and learning data are stored and processed on the Learning Site, which is operated by us. If your organisation or we enable any separate external service (for example a video-meeting or embedded content service), we will tell you, and that service applies its own privacy policy and, where required, asks for your consent first.

1. Information we collect

Account information. When you sign in, the App sends your username and password to the Learning Site. Your account, such as your name, email address, profile picture and the courses you are enrolled in, is created and managed by the Learning Site, usually by your organisation's administrator or when you register.

Learning activity. While you use the App, the Learning Site records your activity in your courses, for example course progress, quiz attempts, grades, assignment submissions, forum posts, messages and calendar events. The App displays this information to you.

Content you choose to provide. If you upload a file, take a photo, record audio or video, or post a message, that content is sent to the Learning Site.

Device and technical information. The App and the Learning Site may record basic technical information, such as device type, operating system version, App version and IP address, to keep the service working and secure.

Notification information. If you allow notifications, a device identifier (push token) may be sent to the Learning Site so that it can deliver notifications to your device.

2. Device permissions

The App asks for permission only when a feature needs it, and you can change these permissions at any time in your device settings.

  • Camera: to take photos or scan QR codes when you choose to.
  • Microphone: to record audio or video when you choose to.
  • Notifications: to tell you about messages, deadlines and course updates.
  • Network access: to connect to the Learning Site and download your course content.

The App does not collect your location, contacts or advertising identifier.

3. How we use your information

  • To let you sign in and use your courses, activities, grades, messages and calendar.
  • To deliver notifications you have enabled.
  • To maintain, secure and improve the App and the Learning Site.
  • To respond to your support requests.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and the App does not show advertising.

4. Information stored on your device

To work faster and to let you view downloaded content offline, the App stores some data on your device, such as your sign-in session, cached course content and files you chose to download. This data stays on your device until you log out, delete the downloaded content or uninstall the App.

5. Sharing of information

Your information is processed on the Learning Site. We share it only:

  • with your organisation or course administrators and teachers, as part of the normal operation of your courses;
  • with service providers who host or support the Learning Site, who are bound to protect it;
  • with notification delivery services of your device platform (for example Google), only if notifications are enabled, and only to deliver them; and
  • with optional external services (for example video-meeting or embedded content tools) that your organisation or we have enabled on the Learning Site. These services have their own privacy policies and, where required, ask for your consent first; and
  • where required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of our users and the platform.

6. Security

Communication between the App and the Learning Site is encrypted using HTTPS. We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, but no system is completely secure. Please keep your password confidential.

7. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the service, meet legal obligations and resolve disputes. After that, it is deleted or anonymised.

8. Your choices and rights

We handle personal data in line with applicable data protection laws, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and, where it applies to you, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to withdraw your consent. You can also change your profile details and notification preferences inside the App.

9. Account and data deletion

To request deletion of your account and the personal data linked to it, email us at officehelp@meetcs.com from the email address registered on your account, with the subject "Account deletion request", or contact your organisation's administrator. We will confirm your request and complete it within 30 days, except for information we must keep by law.

You can remove the data stored on your device at any time by logging out of the App or uninstalling it.

10. Children

The App is intended for learners and professionals. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted at this page with a new effective date. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
