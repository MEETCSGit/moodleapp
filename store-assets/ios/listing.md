### Description (max 4000)

```
Vector LMS brings your learning to your iPhone. Sign in with your Vector Consulting Group account and pick up your courses wherever you are.

- See all your courses in one place, with a clear progress bar for each one
- Open lessons, activities and quizzes in a clean, easy-to-read layout
- Check your calendar for deadlines and events
- Review your grades, files and badges from your account menu
- Download content to study offline
- Read the site blog

Vector LMS works with your organisation's learning site, so you need an account to sign in. If you don't have one, contact your administrator.
```
